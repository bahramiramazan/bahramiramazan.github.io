**[YAR](https://bamaa.org/en)** is an AI-driven online learning platform developed to transform education for students in Afghanistan by addressing severe educational barriers, limited access to qualified instructors, and remote learning challenges.

---

### Core Highlights

* **Generative AI Tutor:** Integrates Generative AI to provide step-by-step problem-solving, immediate explanations, and adaptive feedback customized for STEM curricula.
* **Personalized & Adaptive Learning:** Dynamically adjusts problem difficulty (easy, moderate, advanced) based on individual learner performance to build critical thinking and confidence.
* **Women & Girls Empowerment:** Provides safe, accessible at-home educational tools for female learners facing restricted opportunities, allowing continuous academic growth.
* **Bilingual Support:** Offers full bilingual content and navigation in local languages (Dari and Pashto) to maximize accessibility.

### Impact & Purpose

Traditional educational platforms often fail to account for the unique infrastructural and social constraints in region-specific environments. **YAR** bridges this gap by offering a lightweight, mobile-friendly interface with progress-tracking analytics that allow students to practice independently, test their preparation (such as for Kankor exams), and achieve academic growth.

For more information, visit the official platform at **[bamaa.org/en](https://bamaa.org/en)**.
